IDEA / SOLUTION :
SENTINEL: AI-Powered Intelligence Platform for Real-Time Criminal Network Analysis & Crime Deterrence
• Unified Knowledge Graph : Fuses police FIRs, phone call records (CDRs), bank accounts, and video feeds into one real-time network.
• Kingpin & Boss Discovery : Graph centrality algorithms automatically uncover covert syndicate leaders hiding behind operatives.
• Hawala & AML Detection : Detects 4-node circular money loops (<72h) and smurfing transfers below ₹50,000 threshold.
• Burner Phone Triangulation : Tracks shared mobile handsets (IMEI reuse) and co-located nocturnal meetings (<500m).
• Women Safety Edge AI : Live CCTV monitoring detects stalking and cornering with 1-click Dial 112 PCR dispatch.
• Statutory Evidence Vault : Cryptographic SHA-256 blockchain ledger ensuring 100% Section 63 BSA / 65B IEA court admissibility.

PROBLEM RESOLUTION :
❖ Breaks Data Silos : 
Police usually examine FIRs, phone logs, and bank statements in isolation. SENTINEL automatically merges fragmented clues into an interactive 360° map in seconds.
❖ Exposes Hidden Syndicate Bosses : 
Crime kingpins rarely use their own phones. Advanced Betweenness Centrality and PageRank expose the mastermind brokers coordinating criminal activities.
❖ Stops Crime in Real-Time : 
Shifts law enforcement from slow post-crime investigation to proactive live deterrence—flagging threats and dispatching emergency PCR vans in < 4 minutes.
❖ Guarantees Court Convictions : 
Digital evidence often fails in court due to broken chain of custody. SENTINEL creates Section 63 BSA certified PDF dossiers with SHA-256 blockchain verification.

Frontend Dashboard :
• React 19, TypeScript, Vite – High-performance responsive tactical UI built for police control room command.
• Tailwind CSS – Low-light dark tactical theme reducing officer eye fatigue.
• Cytoscape.js & Leaflet.js – Interactive 2D/3D suspect graph networks and geo-spatial cell tower tracking.
Backend + Intelligence Core :
• FastAPI (Python 3.12) – Ultra-fast asynchronous REST APIs with sub-millisecond query latency.
• NetworkX MultiDiGraph – In-memory graph engine managing 372 nodes, 869 edges and centrality metrics.
• SQLite WAL + Blockchain Hash Chain – Thread-safe data storage with SHA-256 evidence sealing.
• YOLO & spaCy NLP – Real-time gender classification edge AI & automated FIR entity extraction.
Statutory Legal Vault :
• ReportLab Forensic Engine – Generates court-certified Section 63 BSA dossiers with QR code verification.
